Why Make This?

The Problem

When running a TTRPG campaign, player handouts are incredible for engagment, but time consuming to generate. Not every GM is an expert in the various techniques needed to make a convincing in-world prop. It's even more difficult when playing online with people who will only look at things that appear in your virtual tabletop. Arts and crafts skills won't help you there, and pdfs will be ignored.

The Solution

This site allows one to input the content specific to your world, select a layout and quickly get a nicely formatted document to send to your players, or to embed in your VTT of choice.
