TTRPG Handout Generators

Optional domain name: rpg-gen.com

This site provides generators for pre-formatted handouts for use in a TTRPG campaign.

Examples

  • How can I show the goings on in my world in a flavorful, in-universe way? Via a newspaper page!
  • How can I show acquirable items of merchandise to my players in a flavorful, in-universe way? Via a catalogue page!

Formatting

Colors

  • Light #FFFFFF - For Background of text
  • Grey - #a0a0a0 - for secondary backgrounds
  • Dark - #000000 - for final background and text
  • Indigo - #4000FF - Dark Accents
  • Crimson - #FF0040 - Light Accent

Typography

Open Sans - Used for all cases.

The specific generators may use other fonts as needed for the final documents.
